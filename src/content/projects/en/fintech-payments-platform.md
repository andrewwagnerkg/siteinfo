## Context

A fintech startup with 50 employees onboarded business customers manually. Documents arrived by email, and operators tracked checks in spreadsheets. Each new customer took about 3 days.

## Task

"We need to onboard a customer in one day instead of three, without hiring more operators."

## What we did

- Built a web portal where customers upload documents and see the status of their application.
- Built an internal panel for operators with automatic document checks.
- Set up email notifications at every step.
- Moved the system to the cloud with CI/CD and monitoring.

- **Stack:** TypeScript, React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Docker, AWS.
- **Timeline:** 5 months.
- **Team:** 5 people: project manager, designer, 2 developers, QA engineer.

## Result

- Onboarding time: from 3 days to 1 day.
- Manual operations per application: 60% fewer.
- Releases: from once a month to once a week.
