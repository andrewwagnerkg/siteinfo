## Context

A regional logistics company with 300 employees and 120 drivers used paper waybills. Drivers called dispatchers to clarify addresses, and delivery confirmations reached the office only at the end of the day.

## Task

"Drivers should see their route and orders on the phone and confirm delivery on the spot."

## What we did

- Interviewed drivers and dispatchers and tested a clickable prototype in 2 weeks.
- Built an app for iOS and Android with offline mode, barcode scanning and photo proof of delivery.
- Connected the app to the company's existing ERP system through an API.

- **Stack:** React Native, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL.
- **Timeline:** 4 months.
- **Team:** 4 people: designer, 2 developers, QA engineer.

## Result

- Delivery errors: 30% fewer.
- Calls to dispatchers: half as many.
- 95% of drivers use the app every working day.
