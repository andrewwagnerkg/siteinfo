## What you get

- User interviews and analysis of how people work today.
- Clickable prototypes tested before development starts.
- A design system that keeps the interface consistent as the product grows.
- Accessible interfaces that meet WCAG AA.

## Tools

Figma, usability testing, design systems.

## How the work goes

1. Research: users, tasks, current problems.
2. User flows and wireframes.
3. Prototype and usability testing.
4. Final design and handover to developers.
